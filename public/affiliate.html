Who can join?

Anyone 18 or older with a GCash or Maya account: crew members, seafarers, travel bloggers, OFWs, students, travel groups. No sales experience needed.

When they open your website, Messenger or WhatsApp link, or type your code in their first message to us. They stay linked to you for 12 months, so you also earn on their next purchases.

Is a reseller different?

Yes. Resellers buy eSIMs at a lower price and sell them at their own price. Affiliates just share a link and earn a commission, with no buying or delivering. Want to be a reseller? Message us on WhatsApp.

Affiliate terms

The simple rules

  • You earn the commission rate shown on your dashboard (currently 5%) on the amount paid for each order by customers linked to you, after the payment is confirmed.
  • A customer is linked to you for 12 months after they use your link or code, unless they later use another affiliate's link.
  • No commission on your own purchases, on orders by resellers, or on refunded or cancelled orders.
  • Payouts are monthly by GCash or Maya once your balance reaches ₱500. Please keep your payout details correct.
  • Share honestly: no spam, no fake reviews, no misleading claims (e.g. "free data" or wrong prices), and don't run paid ads on the "3UK" name. Don't pretend to be 3UK staff.
  • We may pause an account and withhold commission for fraud or broken rules. We may update these terms; changes apply to future orders.
