FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is an eSIM?

An eSIM is a digital SIM built into your phone. Instead of inserting a plastic SIM card, you scan a QR code.

Does my phone support eSIM?

Most iPhone XS and newer models and most recent Android flagships support eSIM. Message us your phone model and we'll confirm.

Does it work at sea?

Our eSIMs use local mobile networks, so you get data when your ship is in port or sailing near the coast with signal. There's no coverage in the open sea.

I visit many ports. Which plan should I get?

A Global, Asia or Europe plan covers several countries with one eSIM. Message us your route and we'll recommend the best fit.

Can my family buy it for me?

Yes. Family can pay in pesos and we'll send the eSIM QR directly to your email, or to them to forward.

Can our crew or agency order in bulk?

Yes. Message us how many eSIMs you need and your route, and we'll set up the whole crew.

When does my plan start?

Your plan's validity starts when the eSIM first connects to a network at your destination, not on the day you pay.

How do I install it?

iPhone: Settings › Cellular › Add eSIM. Android: Settings › Connections › SIM manager. Install on Wi-Fi before you travel — QR codes usually work only once.

Can I keep my Philippine SIM?

Yes. The travel eSIM is added as a second line, so your Philippine SIM can stay.

How do I pay?

GCash, Maya, MariBank, or bank transfer to UnionBank or BPI.

What if I run out of data?

Just message us — even mid-contract — and we'll set you up with another plan right away.
